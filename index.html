Snap-IT-Out

A New Era of Self-Captured Moments Through a Mobile Photo Booth Experience

A capstone project that tackles the limitations of traditional photo booths (expensive, immobile, and lacking customization) by bringing the full photo booth experience to Android. Snap-IT-Out offers accessibility, creativity, and engagement for users and event organizers alike.

Built with Kotlin and Java, the app features Firebase Authentication, ARCore for augmented reality backgrounds, and OpenCV for image processing. Key features include photobooth-style editing tools, an event mode with customizable templates and guestbooks, collage and mosaic functions, and wireless Wi-Fi printing. Performance testing confirmed high user satisfaction, quick loading times, and optimized resource usage.

HR Manila CORE

SharePoint Intranet Site, ColCap Servicing Pty Ltd, Philippine Branch

A self-serve SharePoint intranet site built for employees to access company policies, guidelines, and announcements in one place.
